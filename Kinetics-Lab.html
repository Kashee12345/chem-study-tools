Each collision's energy is worked out from the two particles' speeds along the line between their centres, on a scale where the average energy matches RT. A reaction happens only if that energy is at least Ea and A's gold side faces B. Activation energies are scaled down (real ones are often 40 to 150 kJ/mol) so you can watch reactions happen in seconds. Each particle stands for 0.010 M.
