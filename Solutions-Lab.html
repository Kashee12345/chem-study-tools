    MeasureValue

    Dilute water solutions: molality is taken as equal to molarity. Kf = 1.86 °C/m, Kb = 0.512 °C/m, R = 0.08206 L·atm/(mol·K). Measured i values are typical values near 0.05 m. The liquid height in the U-tube is drawn on a squeezed scale; a real 1 atm column of water is about 10.3 m tall.
