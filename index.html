General Chemistry · Modules 1 to 17

General Chemistry Study Tools

See chemistry happen. Every module shows each idea three ways at once: what you see in the beaker, what the atoms and molecules are actually doing, and the equation or number that describes it. Walkthroughs include animated worked examples where you watch the units cancel, and every Lab has a simulator, a practice-problem trainer with hints, a game and real-life chemistry. Everything runs in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone. No login, nothing graded.

How to use this: open the walkthrough first and go at your own pace with the arrow keys or Next. Then open the Lab: run the simulator, then practice in the Problem Trainer, using the hints and the animated worked solutions. Use the tools bar at the top of each page to pause the animations, slow them down, have the text read aloud, or get a written description of each picture.
