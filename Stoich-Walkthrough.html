Atoms are drawn as colored spheres with their symbols (hydrogen white, carbon dark grey, nitrogen blue, oxygen red, chlorine green, sodium purple). Molecules are flattened into two dimensions, each drawn particle stands for a huge number of real ones, and reactions are sped up so you can watch them. Atomic masses are rounded table values.
