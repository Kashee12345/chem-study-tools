Part 1: Equilibrium Reactor

Concentrations come from the real rate laws with forward rate = k[reactants] and reverse rate = (k/K)[products], so the mixture always settles where Q = K. Particles are a small sample scaled to the amounts. K values are typical textbook or model values; temperature changes K through the van 't Hoff equation. Reactions are sped up so you can watch them.
